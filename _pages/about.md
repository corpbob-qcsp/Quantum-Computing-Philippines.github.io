## Who We Are

The **Quantum Computing Society of the Philippines (QCSP)**, also known as **OneQuantum Philippines**, is the **national multi-stakeholder quantum community advancing quantum technologies for the benefit of the Philippines**. Founded in 2017, QCSP convenes **government, academe, industry, startups, students, and civil society** around a shared goal: closing the coordination gap in Philippine quantum development by connecting research, talent, policy, and industry adoption into one national ecosystem.

We work to **raise awareness**, **develop local talent**, and **build real-world applications** of quantum technologies for the Philippines. Through **lecture series, hackathons, workshops, conferences, and partnerships** with government, academia, and industry, QCSP helps Filipinos explore how quantum technologies can support **national development, innovation, and responsible adoption**.

---

## What QCSP Is

- An independent national convenor
- A platform for education, collaboration, and public-interest programmes
- A bridge between research, policy, workforce, and adoption
- A credible voice that discloses funding and manages conflicts

---

## What We Do

- **Quantum Public Literacy**  
  A national lecture series, explainers, and public webinars that make quantum computing understandable to students, educators, and the general public.

- **University Capability**  
  Faculty development, curriculum workshops, and quantum roadshows that build teaching and research capacity across Philippine universities.

- **Youth & Inclusion**  
  Scholarships, provincial programmes, and student chapters that widen access to quantum education, especially outside Metro Manila.

- **Quantum-Safe Philippines**  
  Post-quantum cryptography (PQC) awareness, migration briefings, and readiness resources for government, critical infrastructure, and enterprises.

- **Policy & Governance**  
  Evidence-based consultations, policy papers, and horizon scanning that connect regulators, legislators, and researchers.

- **Research & Innovation Network**  
  Researcher mapping, industry-academe matching, and hackathons that connect Filipino talent to real quantum problems and opportunities.

- **QISTCon & National Convening**  
  QCSP's flagship annual Quantum Innovation, Science, and Technology Conference, bringing the entire Philippine quantum ecosystem together.

## Our Vision & Mission

Our vision is a thriving, inclusive quantum ecosystem in the Philippines, contributing as a **founding member of the SEA-QN (Southeast Asia Quantum Network)** to a stronger, connected quantum community across the region.

Our mission is to accomplish this by convening the national quantum community, developing local talent, and connecting the Philippines to regional and global quantum networks — including SEA-QN and **OneQuantum**, the worldwide community of local chapters whose vision of quantum technology as a force for good QCSP shares and helps advance.
