<p class="text-muted text-center mb-4"><em>Director, Innovation, QCSP</em></p>

<p>Jeffrey Aborot is a leading figure in quantum computing research and development in the Philippines. He currently serves as the project lead for the Quantum Circuit Simulation Project at the Department of Science and Technology - Advanced Science and Technology Institute (DOST-ASTI). This pioneering initiative aims to provide local researchers with access to quantum circuit simulation software packages through the Computing and Archiving Research Environment's High-Performance Computing facility.</p>

<p>Under Aborot's leadership, the project strives to enable Filipino researchers to design and analyze quantum algorithms for small problem instances, fostering the growth of quantum computing expertise in the country. His work is instrumental in bridging the gap between theoretical quantum concepts and practical applications in the Philippine context.</p>

<p>Aborot is actively involved in promoting quantum computing education and awareness. He has participated in various conferences and workshops, including the Philippine Computing Society Congress 2023, where he presented on ASTI's projects and the government's efforts in quantum computing.</p>

<p>As a respected voice in the field, Aborot collaborates with academic institutions and industry partners to advance quantum computing research and development in the Philippines. His expertise and leadership are helping to position the country at the forefront of this transformative technology.</p>
