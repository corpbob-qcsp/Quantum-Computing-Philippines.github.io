<p class="text-muted text-center mb-4"><em>Security Professional based in Germany &middot; QCSP</em></p>

<p>An embedded product security professional with more than 15 years of experience in Europe. His current innovation interests lie in Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) and its application to securing embedded systems. His background in cryptography extends to integrating secure protocols for credit cards, passports, and national IDs at a leading German semiconductor company and at Oberthur (now Idemia), a French company.</p>

<p>Earl holds a Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering from the University of the Philippines, following the completion of his sophomore year at the National Institute of Physics at the same university, and an MBA from a joint program of the University of Augsburg and the University of Pittsburgh.</p>
