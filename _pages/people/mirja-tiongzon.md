<p>Mirja Marie Tiongzon is a Filipino educator and teacher at the Department of Education (DepEd) since 2015. She holds a Bachelor's degree in Secondary Education from the University of San Carlos (2014) and a Master of Education in Physics from the University of the Philippines (2018). She is a member of the Quantum Computing Society of the Philippines (QCSP), working on their proposed quantum education program for elementary and high school students.</p>
