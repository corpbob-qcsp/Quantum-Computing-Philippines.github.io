<p class="text-muted text-center mb-4"><em>CTO, Post-Quantum Full-Stack Agentic AI Systems</em></p>

<p>Edison Macabebe is a CTO, principal systems engineer, architect, and technology leader with experience in DevOps, AIOps, MLOps, and artificial-intelligence systems. His work includes full-stack agentic AI, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and Model Context Protocol (MCP)-based solutions, with a background spanning AI systems design, platform engineering, and technical architecture. He also brings experience in product and program management, helping guide the development of complex AI-driven systems from concept through deployment across both enterprise and emerging quantum-adjacent technology environments.</p>
