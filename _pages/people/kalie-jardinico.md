<p class="text-muted text-center mb-4"><em>Member, Machine Intelligence and Neural Dynamics (MIND) Laboratory, UP Baguio &middot; Research Fellow, QCSP</em></p>

<p>A BS Physics graduate from the University of the Philippines Baguio and a member of the MIND Laboratory. Her undergraduate research focuses on Quantum Key Distribution (QKD), particularly a teleportation-based protocol, where she establishes baseline performance metrics and benchmarks against established protocols such as BB84 and E91, alongside analyzing the effects of noise and quantum attacks on system security.</p>

<p>She is a Research Fellow of the Quantum Computing Society of the Philippines (QCSP) and previously served as a Student Intern at the Quantum Innovation Laboratory (QIL) under the DOST Advanced Science and Technology Institute (DOST-ASTI). She has published an international paper under the IEEE, is a recipient of the National Research Council of the Philippines (NRCP) Young Scientist Research Grant, and is part of the team awarded the Gawad Pangulo para sa Natatanging Mag-aaral (Gawad Pangulo for Student Innovations) for &pi;Flux, a next-generation cybersecurity solution integrating Physics-Informed Artificial Intelligence.</p>
