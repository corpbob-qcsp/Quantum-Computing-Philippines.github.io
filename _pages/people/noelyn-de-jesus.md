<p class="text-muted text-center mb-4"><em>Associate Professor, College of Informatics and Computing Sciences, Batangas State University ARASOF-Nasugbu &middot; Director, Marketing &amp; Relations, QCSP</em></p>

<p>Dr. Noelyn M. De Jesus is an Associate Professor at the College of Informatics and Computing Sciences, Batangas State University ARASOF-Nasugbu. She holds a Doctor in Information Technology and is actively engaged in teaching, research, and extension services. Her scholarly work centers on the application of artificial intelligence, machine learning, data analytics, and intelligent systems across diverse domains.</p>

<p>Her research contributions span predictive modeling, deep learning, decision-support systems, technology adoption, and optimization-driven analytics. She has authored and co-authored studies involving neural network-based forecasting, spatiotemporal machine learning models, AI-driven decision frameworks, educational technology acceptance, tourism analytics, and data-driven performance monitoring systems.</p>

<p>Her interdisciplinary research reflects a strong focus on leveraging computational intelligence to address complex real-world challenges in energy systems, education, tourism, manufacturing, and organizational information systems. Dr. De Jesus continues to pursue research initiatives that integrate artificial intelligence with applied analytics, emphasizing predictive intelligence, system efficiency, and data-driven decision-making.</p>
