<p>Mariana Filipova is a Bulgarian quantum computing educator and entrepreneur based in Sofia. She is Founder &amp; CEO of STEALM Academy, a PhD student at the University of Library Studies and Information Technologies (ULSIT), and a specialist in corporate quantum training at Swastikk AI Tech. She actively organizes QWorld QBronze workshops and focuses on quantum algorithms, architecture, and cryptography.</p>
