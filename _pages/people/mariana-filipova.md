<p>Dr. Mariana Filipova is the Founder and CEO of STEALM Academy in Bulgaria and the Coordinator of QWomen at QWorld. An educator and trainer, she brings quantum technology education to young learners and partners with the Quantum Computing Society of the Philippines to reach elementary and high school students. She completed her doctoral studies in Earth and Space Quantum Communication Technologies at the University of Library Studies and Information Technologies in Sofia, where she also earned a master's degree in Computer Software Engineering, and she holds a bachelor's degree in Pedagogical Sciences from Sofia University St. Kliment Ohridski.</p>
