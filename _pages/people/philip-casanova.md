<p class="text-muted text-center mb-4"><em>Principal, SGV &amp; Co. (EY Philippines) &middot; President, ISC2 Philippines Chapter</em></p>

<p>Principal in SGV &amp; Co. (Ernst &amp; Young Philippines) and the firm&rsquo;s cybersecurity consulting leader, and President of the International Information System Security Certification Consortium (ISC2) Philippines Chapter. He brings over 30 years of experience as a consultant and Chief Information Security Officer, with leadership roles in financial services across New York City, the Asia region, India, and the Philippines.</p>

<p>One of the first Certified Information Systems Security Professionals (CISSP) in the Philippines, since 2002, he is also a Certified Information Security Manager (CISM), Certified in Risk and Information Systems Control (CRISC), and a Member of the Business Continuity Institute (MBCI). He is pursuing a Doctorate in Information Technology with research interest in cybersecurity reform and quantum-safe adoption.</p>
