<p class="text-muted text-center mb-4"><em>Founder and CEO, DUMATEK</em></p>

<p>A Filipino-American technology entrepreneur and cybersecurity specialist with approximately three decades of experience in information technology, including service in the United States Navy. He is the founder and CEO of DUMATEK, a cybersecurity company providing solutions in ransomware protection and managed detection and response, and helping Philippine critical information infrastructures achieve proper and compliant operational technology (OT) security. His work focuses on strengthening the cybersecurity capabilities of Philippine organizations, particularly in energy, manufacturing, water, oil and gas, food and beverages, telecommunications, and logistics.</p>

<p>Beyond conventional cybersecurity, he has a strong interest in quantum computing. In 2022 he led the introduction of quantum computing education as an elective at La Salle Green Hills, taught by IBM and MIT instructors.</p>
