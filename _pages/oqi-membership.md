The **Quantum Computing Society of the Philippines (QCSP) is a Member of the Open Quantum Institute (OQI)**.

QCSP remains committed to enhancing the global competitiveness of Filipinos through quantum computing technologies by empowering learners with world-class, industry-standard training.

QCSP’s membership in OQI does not imply CERN’s endorsement of QCSP or its activities, products or services.
