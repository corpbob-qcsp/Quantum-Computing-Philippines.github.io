A suggested reading order for QCSP's quantum computing blog series, from first principles to hands-on implementation. Several posts build directly on the one before them, so within each track, start at the top and work down.

## Foundations

1. [Introducing Quantum Computing](/introduction-to-quantum-computing/) — a plain-language starting point for the field.
2. [Think of a Number: How to Program a Quantum Computer](/think-of-a-number-howto-program-quantum-computer/) — a simple puzzle that introduces how quantum programs work.
3. [Quantum Computing: Programming the Quantum Dice](/programming-quantum-dice/) — simulating randomness using qubit superposition.

## Quantum Search

4. [Quantum Searching](/quantum-searching/) — how Grover's search works, worked out by hand.
5. [Programming the Quantum Search](/programming-quantum-search/) — the same algorithm, implemented in Qiskit.

## Cryptography — RSA Track

6. [How RSA Encryption Works](/how-rsa-works/) — classical RSA encryption, no quantum computer required.
7. [Period Finding and the RSA](/period-finding-rsa/) — the number-theory trick quantum computers exploit.
8. [RSA Encryption and Quantum Computing](/rsa-encryption-quantum-computing/) — the theory behind cracking RSA with a quantum computer.

## Cryptography — Elliptic Curve Track

9. [Elliptic Curve Cryptography](/ellipticurve-cryptography/) — classical elliptic curve cryptography.
10. [Quantum Computing and Elliptic Curve Cryptography](/quantum-computing-ellipticurve-cryptography/) — the quantum attack on elliptic curve keys.

## Cryptography — Capstone

11. [Constructing the Quantum Fourier Circuit](/constructing-quantum-fourier-transform/) — building the QFT circuit from scratch.
12. [Programming Shor's Algorithm to Crack the RSA Cryptography](/programming-shors-algorithm/) — bringing RSA, period finding, and the QFT circuit together in Qiskit.

## Optimization: Ising Model &amp; VQE

13. [I Sing Quantum Computing](/ising-quantum-computing/) — an introduction to the Ising Model for NP-complete problems.
14. [Traveling Salesman and Quantum Computing](/traveling-salesman-quantum-computing/) — mapping the Traveling Salesman Problem onto the Ising Model.
15. [Introducing the Variational Quantum Eigensolver](/intro-variational-quantum-eigensolver/) — a heuristic for tackling problems too large to solve exactly.

## Advanced Application

16. [Computing State Vector of Probability Distributions](/computing-state-of-probability-distribution/) — deriving a generic circuit for probability distribution functions.
