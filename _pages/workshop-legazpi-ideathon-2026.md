The **Quantum Technology and Computing Ideathon** (also referred to as the Quantum Computing Ideathon) runs as the afternoon program of the **1st R-IT Congress: Region V**, themed "Future-Proofing IT: Building a Resilient Future in an AI-Centric World." The Ideathon itself carries its own theme: **"Quantum Solutions for National Development."**

Unlike a traditional programming hackathon, this event is designed to develop innovative concepts, business use cases, and policy proposals using quantum-inspired, hybrid, or quantum technology. **No prior quantum programming experience is required.**

The Ideathon is organized by Invictus. Training. Solutions. in partnership with the Quantum Computing Society of the Philippines (QCSP), Salesforce, EC-Council, and Certiport, with the Computing Society of Sorsogon State University&ndash;Bulan Campus supporting as a local partner.

### Competition Tracks

Interdisciplinary teams (up to 8 groups) select one of four announced tracks:

- **Track 1** &mdash; Quantum Computing Applications
- **Track 2** &mdash; Quantum-Safe Cybersecurity
- **Track 3** &mdash; Quantum for Government and Public Services
- **Track 4** &mdash; Quantum Entrepreneurship
