This half-day **webinar** helps organizations prepare for the post-quantum era. It starts with a short introduction to quantum computing and why it threatens today's public-key cryptography, then moves to **post-quantum cryptography (PQC)**, how organizations are approaching its **adoption**, what it means **inside the enterprise**, and the specific challenge of **securing operational technology (OT) infrastructure**.

The sessions are given by QCSP and invited practitioners from academia, consulting, and industry.
