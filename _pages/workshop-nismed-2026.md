This one-day **Quantum Computing Workshop**, hosted at the **National Institute for Science and Mathematics Education Development (UP NISMED)**, gives science and mathematics educators a practical, grounded introduction to quantum computing and how it can be brought into the classroom.

The program begins with the foundations of quantum computing&mdash;including superposition, entanglement, tunneling, interference, and measurement&mdash;before progressing to **computational complexity, quantum circuits and algorithms, and hands-on programming using Qiskit**. Participants will gain practical experience building and running quantum circuits while developing an understanding of where quantum computers may offer advantages over classical approaches.

The day closes with a dedicated focus on **quantum education for K&ndash;12**, covering how to introduce quantum concepts to elementary and high school learners and drawing on international lessons from STEALM Academy's classroom experience in Bulgaria.
