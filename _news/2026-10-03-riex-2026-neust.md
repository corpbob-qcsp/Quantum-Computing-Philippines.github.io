The **International Conference and Workshop on Emerging Technologies and Innovation Ecosystems 2026 (RIEx 2026)** will take place on **November 18–19, 2026** at the **NEUST Mini Convention Center**, with an online option. The conference carries the theme *"Fostering Global Partnerships for Research Excellence, Technological Advancement, and Sustainable Futures."*

RIEx 2026 brings together researchers, educators, students, industry professionals, government personnel, and innovators on a platform that promotes research, emerging technologies, innovation, collaboration, and knowledge exchange.

## What to Expect

- **Conference** — gain insights from experts and thought leaders.
- **Hackathon** — a Quantum Computing and Cybersecurity Hackathon.
- **Research and Extension Project Presentations** — showcase innovative research and extension initiatives.
- **Networking** — build meaningful partnerships and collaborations.
- **Innovation and Knowledge Exchange** — share ideas and explore solutions to real-world challenges.

## QCSP's Role

The Quantum Computing Society of the Philippines (QCSP) is a co-organizer of RIEx 2026 and is helping bring quantum computing and cybersecurity into the program, including the Quantum Computing and Cybersecurity Hackathon.

The collaboration builds on QCSP's earlier work with NEUST: in June 2026, [NEUST hosted the Luzon leg](/news/2026-06-06-neust-quantum-cybersecurity-hackathon/) of the nationwide Quantum Computing and Cybersecurity Hackathon, organized by QCSP and the ISC2 Philippines Chapter.

## Registration Fees

| Participant type | Fee |
|---|---|
| Face-to-Face Participant | ₱3,500 |
| Research & Extension Presenter | ₱2,000 |
| Non-Presenting Participant | ₱1,500 |
| Student | ₱800 |

The Face-to-Face Participant fee includes conference meals only; accommodation and breakfast are not included.
