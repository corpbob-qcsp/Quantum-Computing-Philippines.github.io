The final round of the Quantathon on Quantum Computing 2026 officially opened on July 24, 2026, at the International Centre for Interdisciplinary Science and Education (ICISE) in Quy Nhon, Vietnam.

| ![](/assets/images/vnquantum_00.JPG) |
|:--:|
| *The opening ceremony of QC4SG Vietnam – The 2nd SEA Quantathon, held at ICISE in Quy Nhon.* |

The international competition, also known as QC4SG 2026, brought together students, researchers, quantum technology experts, industry representatives, and innovation leaders to develop solutions using quantum computing. The event emphasized the role of international cooperation, education, and innovation in building a sustainable quantum technology ecosystem.

The opening ceremony featured representatives from the Gia Lai Provincial People's Committee and Vietnam's National Innovation Center. Discussions highlighted the importance of creating networks that connect experts, institutions, businesses, and young talent to advance quantum technologies for sustainable development.

ICISE representatives emphasized the value of global scientific connections in strengthening research cooperation and innovation. International quantum technology organizations also shared perspectives on the growing quantum ecosystem, including Quandela of France, Qudora of Germany, and Quapp of Japan.

The presentations explored several important areas, including the development of quantum computing and its potential applications in Vietnam's strategic sectors; the infrastructure and platforms needed to support research, training, and innovation; and the importance of connecting talented individuals with businesses and real-world challenges to encourage the growth of quantum startups.

Following the ceremonial launch of QC4SG 2026, VNQuantum presented the competition's orientation, rules, and principal challenges. Finalists were then prepared to apply their knowledge and creativity to problems demonstrating how quantum technologies can contribute to society and sustainable development.

The Quantum Computing Society of the Philippines (QCSP), a co-organizer of the 2026 Vietnam Quantathon together with VNQuantum, supported the event as part of its commitment to advancing quantum education and strengthening regional collaboration. QCSP's involvement also reflects the growing connection between the Philippine and Vietnamese quantum communities.

Quantathon 2026 demonstrates how countries in Southeast Asia can work together to nurture young quantum talent, expand access to emerging technologies, and build a regional community capable of contributing to the rapidly developing global quantum ecosystem.
